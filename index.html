# Privacy Policy — AgileFlow AI **Last Updated: October 23, 2026** ## 1. About AgileFlow AI AgileFlow AI — Human-in-the-Loop Multi-Agent Framework for Agile SDLC Automation is an academic research prototype developed as part of a university final-year Software Engineering research project. The system investigates how human-supervised intelligent agents can assist with software requirements engineering, design and planning, implementation, testing, validation, and related Agile software-development activities. ## 2. Jira Integration AgileFlow AI provides an optional integration with Atlassian Jira Cloud. The Jira integration uses OAuth 2.0 authorization. Users must explicitly authorize AgileFlow AI before the system can access permitted Jira resources. AgileFlow AI does not request or collect users' Atlassian passwords. ## 3. Information Accessed Depending on the permissions granted by the user, the Jira integration may access information required to provide its functionality, including: - Jira sites accessible to the authorized account; - Jira projects and project identifiers; - Jira Software boards and sprints; - Jira issue types and related project configuration; - Jira issues required for synchronization and traceability; and - identifiers and metadata required to maintain the authorized Jira connection. Access is limited to the functionality required by the research prototype and the OAuth permissions granted by the user. ## 4. Information Stored AgileFlow AI may store OAuth authorization information, including access and refresh tokens, and Jira connection metadata required to maintain the authorized integration. The system may also store identifiers that map AgileFlow AI planning artifacts to Jira resources, such as Jira project, board, sprint, and issue identifiers, for synchronization and traceability. The research prototype is not intended to collect Atlassian account passwords. ## 5. How Information Is Used Information accessed through Jira is used only to provide AgileFlow AI functionality, including: - discovering Jira sites, projects, boards, sprints, and issue types; - presenting synchronization previews; - synchronizing user-approved Agile planning artifacts with Jira; - creating Jira issues and sprints after explicit user confirmation; and - maintaining traceability between AgileFlow AI artifacts and Jira resources. ## 6. Human Approval AgileFlow AI follows a human-in-the-loop approach. Generating a Jira synchronization plan does not automatically modify Jira Cloud. Users are provided with an opportunity to review the synchronization plan and must explicitly confirm synchronization before Jira resources are created or modified. ## 7. Data Sharing AgileFlow AI does not sell personal information. Information accessed through the Jira integration is used for the operation and evaluation of the academic research prototype and is not intentionally provided to third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. ## 8. Data Retention and Revocation Jira authorization information and integration metadata are retained only as required to support the authorized connection and research prototype functionality. Users may revoke AgileFlow AI's access through their Atlassian account. Research participants may also contact the research team regarding removal of information maintained by the prototype where applicable. ## 9. Security Reasonable technical measures are used to protect Jira integration credentials and application data. OAuth credentials must not be intentionally exposed in client-side code, public repositories, or application logs. As AgileFlow AI is an academic research prototype, it should not be treated as a production service for storing highly sensitive or confidential information. ## 10. Research Purpose AgileFlow AI is currently developed and evaluated for academic research, demonstration, and testing purposes. It is not currently offered as a commercial production service. ## 11. Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated as the research prototype, integrations, or data-handling practices change. The latest version will be published at this location. ## 12. Contact Questions about this Privacy Policy or the AgileFlow AI research prototype may be directed to the AgileFlow AI research team through the contact information provided with the application.
